# Functional evidence for the eleven discovery targets

No qualifying candidate-specific post-immune basal/stimulated secretion comparison was located within the specified search. Among the **six scored human counterparts**, direct baseline assays were located for **two** (HIVEP2 and RNLS), represented by **seven model-study comparisons**. Some assays show a glucose response, including Cai's RNLS-edited human SC-beta cells. These observations are credited as baseline evidence independently of whether a formal preservation analysis was performed. **0/6 adequate post-immune comparisons is an evidence-coverage count, not a biological failure rate.** Bode2023's full primary text and complete official supplement were assessed; no direct secretion or glucose-function endpoint was located.

The eleven-target functional assessment does not change the six-member quantitative cohort. The original **32 comparisons (27 candidate and five interpretation-control records)** retain their identities and values except for source-supported clarification of FE018's output units and observed response, logged in `evidence/functional_revision3_corrections.json`. FE033–FE037 are five literature-coverage records, not experiments. FE038 is a completed full-text Bode2023 source-assessment record, not an extracted experiment. FE039–FE040 add two Bode2024 immune comparisons. Thus the table contains **40 records: 34 extracted comparisons, five coverage records and one completed full-text source assessment**. Repeated entries from a pooled screen are not independent replication; reused Bode2024 mouse controls are identified.

`evidence/functional_matrix.tsv` gives eleven-target measurement coverage. `evidence/baseline_experiments.tsv` gives seven gene-model-study rows with observed response, control response, output basis, comparison and immune timing. Neither assigns a gene-wide function grade. Full curation is in `evidence/functional_evidence.tsv` and `.csv`; root `functional_evidence.tsv` is identical.

## Search and inclusion methods

The initial search was conducted on **2026-10-24**, with a five-target extension and subsequent **uniform eleven-target search on 2026-10-25** (America/New_York). Earlier logs and raw inputs remain intact. Before the uniform publication queries, `protocol/functional_search_v3.json` fixed every strategy, alias source, date boundary, eligibility rule and retrieval cap. Each query combined a title/abstract alias disjunction with identical insulin/islet/beta-cell context and secretion/function/glucose/GSIS/protection/immunity blocks. Official human and mouse symbols, descriptions and aliases came from **21 NCBI Gene authority records**, with original discovery labels retained. Broad LGS and GLUR2/mGluR7 aliases were allowed for retrieval and individually checked for target identity; their collisions were not treated as candidate evidence.

Europe PMC core metadata searches imposed no lower date limit and an upper `FIRST_PDATE` of **2026-10-25**. The declared cap was **1,000 records per target**, in the database's default order; no cap was reached. Counts for HIVEP2, RNLS, GCN1, MED11, ZBED3, ZNF28, LGSN, PLIN4, TGM6, GRM2 and Or8b47 were **4,12,0,0,9,0,42,16,4,22,0**, respectively: **109 target-record memberships and 107 unique source identifiers**. Every returned title and available abstract was screened; plausible exact-target experiments were followed to accessible primary text and supplements. Three preprint/publication pairs did not duplicate experiments. Exact strategies and all decisions are in `evidence/literature_search.json` and `evidence/literature_uniform_screening.tsv`. Frozen metadata, authority records and primary follow-ups are in `data/raw/literature/uniform20261025/`, with exact URL, retrieval UTC, size and SHA256 commitments.

The separately frozen `protocol/revision3_citation_plan.json` checked one backward and one forward citation generation around Cai2020, Sintov2022, Karaoglu2026 and MacDonald2025. It retrieved **389 indexed edges** (263 references and 126 citing edges). Reconciliation against **270 XML bibliography entries** added five entries missing from the endpoint and preserved two repeated Cai identifiers. After deduplication, **353 records** were screened; core metadata were available for 348, with 335 indexed abstracts plus one DOI-resolved abstract. No per-anchor/direction cap of 1,000 was reached. Relevant candidate and beta-cell intervention leads received primary follow-up; no recursive citation expansion was performed. Citation and uniform searches overlap, so their record counts cannot be added. Every disposition and access gap is in `evidence/citation_search_log.json`.

Qualifying direct functional evidence requires an identifiable candidate perturbation in beta cells/islets and a source-resolved secreted-insulin measurement with explicit baseline or immune timing. Protection without secretion is retained separately. Species, cell product, edit verification, immune effectors and replication remain distinct. Expression or insulin-content proxies, systemic-only metabolism, different paralogs, nonselective receptor drugs and unrelated tissues are not exact-edit secretion evidence. Natural variants can be informative, but association alone does not demonstrate the effect of the nominated loss-of-function edit. No systematic-review completeness, universal absence or priority claim is made. The reporting draws on [PRISMA2020](https://doi.org/10.1136/bmj.n71) and [PRISMA-S](https://doi.org/10.1186/s13643-020-01542-z) without claiming full compliance.

Evidence states are kept separate: **M**, a direct assay was measured; **NL**, no qualifying comparison located within this search; **NM**, the endpoint was not reported in an assessed experiment; and **AC**, evidence against a specified model-specific claim. A comparison can be inconclusive for preservation without erasing an observed glucose response. **Unresolved access**, when present, means the available material cannot establish whether an endpoint was measured; the completed Bode2023 assessment has no such access gap. None is a gene-wide biological grade.

## Retained function: comparator and measurement specification

A retention claim must name its reference. Four comparisons answer different questions and should be reported distinctly:

1. **Postchallenge versus prechallenge within the edited product:** longitudinal preservation, with time-matched unchallenged controls to separate maturation, drift and repeated-assay effects.
2. **Challenged edit versus matched unchallenged edit at the same time:** the effect of immune exposure on that product, preferably accounting for baseline differences.
3. **Challenged edit versus challenged WT/non-targeting product:** relative benefit from the edit under exposure. Benefit over a severely damaged control alone does not establish useful retained output.
4. **Postchallenge output versus a prospectively justified minimum useful output:** whether the surviving product meets its intended function. A clinical threshold cannot be imported into these preclinical assays without justification.

Report **total absolute graft/product insulin output**, **surviving beta-cell mass or number**, and **secretion per surviving beta cell** separately. A per-cell ratio can remain high while most total graft capacity is lost. Basal and stimulated absolute outputs, stimulated-minus-basal or other declared glucose-response contrast, and stimulation ratio should be interpretable together. Normalizing by DNA, insulin content or total cell count answers different questions and cannot silently replace viable beta-cell mass. In vivo serum insulin concentration also depends on distribution and clearance; it is not automatically a secretion flux.

For a formal noninferiority or equivalence claim, declare the functional endpoint, comparator, effect scale, biologically justified acceptable loss/equivalence bounds, analysis unit, challenge time and uncertainty procedure before interpretation. The corresponding confidence interval must satisfy the declared criterion; a nonsignificant difference is insufficient ([Lakens 2017](https://doi.org/10.1177/1948550617697177)). No numerical margin is invented here. Without an appropriate comparator, responsive control, recoverable measurements and a justified criterion, retention remains unestablished.

## Supplementary study-specific taxonomy

F0–F4 is a local evidence checklist, **not a validated scale**, biological rank, or immutable gene property. The main matrix exposes the underlying dimensions directly.

| Label | Definition for a source-resolved comparison |
|---|---|
| F0 | No direct candidate-specific secreted-insulin assay in that comparison; expression/content/survival proxies do not qualify |
| F1 | Direct secretion measured before or without immune challenge; does not imply adequate control response or retained function |
| F2 | Direct post-immune secreted-insulin output measured, but adequate contemporaneous basal/stimulated assessment absent |
| F3 | Adequate post-immune basal/stimulated assessment with interpretable absolute output and responsive matched control; retention claim not yet supported |
| F4 | F3 plus a clearly named functional reference and justified, prospectively declared retention criterion supported by an aligned estimate and uncertainty interval; total output and viable beta-cell mass/per-cell secretion distinguished |

No original primary-candidate record reaches F2–F4. The CXCL10 post-PBMC output record is F2, not F3/F4. Supplemental literature-coverage rows have no F grade because they are not experimental comparisons. Exact secretion values and intervals have not been reconstructed from bar heights. Graph-only values are marked not numerically extracted; missing data are never imputed as preserved function.

## Joint evidence requirement and next experiment

The joint requirement is independently validated protection against autoimmune and allogeneic exposure, plus adequate postchallenge function for each relevant setting, or a valid combined-challenge experiment. It is not established for any of the six scored candidates within the assessed sources: adequate post-immune function was not located in the assessed primary text and supplements. The expanded eleven-target map likewise locates no qualifying joint demonstration. These are coverage statements, not efficacy estimates.

A discriminating next experiment would use one demonstrably glucose-responsive human beta-cell product, independently verified edits or clones, unchallenged controls and separate autoimmune and allogeneic challenge arms. Replicate across differentiation batches and relevant immune donors; technical wells are not biological replicates. Jointly assess survival, total absolute output and surviving-cell glucose response before and after exposure where feasible, using the references above. Analyze edit-by-challenge contrasts directly. GCN1 and HIVEP2 could resolve uncertain positive screen directions; RNLS provides a context-dependence benchmark; a separately validated allogeneic comparator can test whether the challenge detects protection. This is a rationale for resolving uncertainty, not an endorsement of a successful edit.

The contribution is a defined nominated-set comparison, transparent eligibility accounting and an experiment-specific map of missing functional evidence. Known RNLS context dependence and CXCL10/STAT1 findings remain prior art. The bounded search does not certify priority. Frozen sources and source-specific locators accompany the records.
